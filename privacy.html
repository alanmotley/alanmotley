Privacy.

Last updated June 9, 2026.

Overview

alanmotley.com is a personal professional website used to share background, projects, writing, and contact links. This page summarizes the basic analytics and contact practices used on the site.

Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity to understand broad site usage, traffic sources, page interaction, scroll depth, clicks, and user-experience issues such as dead clicks or layout friction.

Microsoft Clarity may create heatmaps and session recordings. Clarity masks sensitive text by default. Analytics information is used to improve the site and understand which pages or links are useful.

Contact

If you contact Alan through an email or external profile link, the information you send is handled through that external service or email provider.

Choices

You can use browser privacy controls, tracker-blocking tools, or analytics opt-out tools to limit analytics collection. Microsoft provides more information in its privacy statement.
